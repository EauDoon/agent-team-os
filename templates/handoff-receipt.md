# Handoff receipt

## Identity and revision

Record the task ID, assignment ID, sender, recipient, brief revision, message ID
and correlation ID. Identify the exact output revision or digest reviewed.

## Output and acceptance

List the delivered artifacts and their locations. For each output-contract
criterion, record pass, fail or unverified with the evidence or check used.
Distinguish sender completion from recipient acceptance.

## Evidence and gaps

List inspected input revisions and relevant claim IDs. Preserve assumptions,
contradictions and missing evidence. State any decision needed to proceed.

## Effects and ownership

List completed writes and external effects, if any, with their existing
authorization. Record who owns each artifact now. Identify any running work
that must stop before ownership can transfer.

## Receipt decision

Choose accepted, correction required or blocked. Give the reason and the
receiving owner's name. For corrections, name the exact criteria to recheck.

## Resume checkpoint

Record the last accepted output, pending work, original authorization and the
event required to resume. On resumption, compare current inputs and output
revisions with this receipt. Revalidate changed assumptions and authorization;
do not infer renewed access from the existence of a checkpoint.
