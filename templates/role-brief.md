# Role brief

Use one brief for one task-scoped role. Keep the access scope no broader than
the task requires. Complete every field before delegation.

## Role

Name the temporary responsibility and the decision or artifact it owns.

## Access scope

List the exact sources, tools, actions, and read or write limits. State what is
out of scope.

## Task

State the distinct question, artifact, or risk this role owns.

## Evidence

List the inputs to inspect and the traceability required for every material
claim. Mark missing evidence as a gap.

## Output contract

Specify the format, contents, quality bar, recipient, and status labels.

## Stop condition

State the event that ends work, including completion, a blocking gap, a scope
conflict, or diminishing returns.

## Safety check

Before delegation, confirm that the brief contains no implicit authority,
unbounded access, hidden external action, or instruction to suppress evidence.
